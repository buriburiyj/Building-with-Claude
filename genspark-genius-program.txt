Genspark Genius Program (summary)

Overview
- Global community of passionate Genspark users who lead local chapters,
  host events, and share real-world use cases.
- Volunteer-style community role (not a paid job).

Benefits
- Official "Genius" title
- Plus Plan access with free credits (premium Genspark product access)
- Funding for community events (venue, food, materials)
- Branded templates and presentation assets
- Shoutouts on Genspark community channels and social media
- Private network of Geniuses worldwide
- Direct line to the Genspark team (regular calls, ongoing mentorship)

Eligibility / Participation Criteria
- No developer or AI-expert background required
- Passion for Genspark, initiative, and ability to bring people together
- Community influence (content, meetups, events) and willingness to keep
  investing time in building a local chapter
- Small cities and online/topic- or industry-specific communities are welcome
- Time commitment: about 10-20 hours per month, flexible

How to Apply
- Fill out a ~10 minute form
- If a good fit, you are invited to a 30-minute conversation
- Whole process usually takes 2-4 weeks
